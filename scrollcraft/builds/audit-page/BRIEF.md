# BRIEF — /audit landing page rebuild

Interviewed 2026-09-02 (Max, in-session). Answers verbatim below.
Build model: Fable 5 (claude-fable-5) — this build doubles as a live cost/performance test.

## The eight answers

1. **Vibe:** "sharp, credible, no-fluff, diagnostic. References — not sites, but objects:
   a lab result letter, a credit score reveal, an X-ray light-table. This is a diagnostic
   product; lean into that instead of generic 'premium.'"
2. **Journey:** "Recognition (your site has AI blind spots you can't see) → Tension (what
   that costs you, plainly) → Turn (the audit reveals it) → Substance (real sample findings,
   real score rings) → Commitment (drop your URL, get the audit free)."
3. **Energy curve:** "calm and serious at open (this is a real diagnostic, not a gimmick) →
   rises through the reveal of the score/findings → settles back down at the CTA so
   submitting feels low-friction, not a hard sell."
4. **Feeling / peak:** "the peak is watching your own score assemble in real time — the ring
   filling in, the number landing. 'It's the page where you watch your AI-readiness score
   get diagnosed in front of you.'"
5. **The one unique thing:** "scroll-driven reveal of a real sample audit — not a mockup, an
   actual finding from an actual run — with the score ring animating in sync with scroll
   position, like flipping through the real PDF page by page."
6. **Range:** "premium-minimal, matching the homepage's navy/gold system. This page's job is
   converting into a $150/hr consulting relationship — too playful undercuts trust."
7. **World vs scenes:** "distinct scenes/chapters, not one continuous world-flight. This is
   a short conversion page, not a brand story — a full cinematic world is slower to build
   and slower to load, working against the CTA."
8. **Assets:** "real Wex brand tokens + logo already exist from the homepage build — reuse
   those directly. For content, use real screenshots from an actual audit run (score rings,
   sample findings) rather than generated photography — this is a product/data page, not a
   lifestyle page, so I'd flag it as a deliberate deviation from 'photographic world' default."

## Brief (Step 1)

- **What is this, who is it for:** Free AI opportunity audit — small/mid-market business
  owners. Visitor pastes their URL, gets a PDF report with quantified savings.
- **What must the visitor believe by the end:** "This audit will show me, in dollars,
  exactly where AI saves my business money — and it costs me nothing to find out."
- **The one action:** Run my audit. (Label used everywhere: "Analyze My Business".)
- **Existing assets:** navy/gold brand system (navy #0A1226 / #0F1F3D, gold #C8A84B),
  real audit output from an actual run (25N test run — anonymized as "25-person services
  firm", same anonymization pattern as the /work showcase), existing PrimaryCta styling.
- **Art direction:** no photographic world — deliberate deviation, this is a data/product
  page. The world is a diagnostic instrument surface.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | Caused by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Intake | curiosity, "it's already running" | analysis status log filling line by line under the scanline |
| 2 Cost | discomfort | the manual-labor math accumulating — hours × rate, plainly totalled |
| 3 Turn | relief, recognition | the findings board wiping in — ranked opportunities with real $ figures |
| 4 Score (PEAK) | the number landing | five rings assembling under scroll, $312K counting up, values committing as the scanline crosses them |
| 5 Commit | calm, low friction | a real input field with a cursor in it — the page stops performing and asks one thing |

## The peak

"You scroll and the score rings fill themselves in — then the annual-savings number
lands." Lives in Act 4. Largest scroll span on the page.

## Tell-someone sentence

"It's the page where you watch an AI audit run on a real business while you scroll —
and then it asks for your URL."

## Authored silence

The breath between Act 4 (peak) and Act 5 (input): a short quiet gap so the ask
arrives after the number has landed, not on top of it. This is intentional, not dead
scroll.
