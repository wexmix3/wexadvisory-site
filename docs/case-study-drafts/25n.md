# Case study draft: multi-location coworking operator (25N Coworking)

**Industry:** Flexible coworking · **Footprint:** 5 locations, Chicago and Dallas-Fort Worth metro areas · **Engagement:** competitive analysis, then finance and operations systems

## Problem

The finance team ran month-end close by hand. Each location's general ledger came out of the accounting system (Yardi) as an export, and someone reconciled it in Excel, one location at a time. There was no single view of how each location stood against budget, and occupancy lived in a separate space-management platform that nobody looked at next to the financials.

Close took [NEEDS CLIENT NUMBER: days or hours per month spent on close before the build]. Answering "which location is behind budget this month, and why?" meant building a report first.

Leadership also had no systematic read on how they compared to regional competitors on traffic, reviews or local search.

## Approach

I started with the question leadership could act on fastest: where do we stand against competitors? A competitive analysis covering five direct rivals, local SEO, Google Business Profile health and review velocity was delivered 9 hours after kickoff.

Then I built the finance side, one piece at a time, with the finance team reviewing every round:

- **Live GL dashboard.** Every location's P&L in one place, with revenue and NOI against budget, month-over-month variance flags at the section and account level, a six-month trend, run-rate projections for partial months, and a health badge per location. AI writes a short plain-English summary for each location every time new data lands.
- **GL check.** Per-account rules (control numbers, vendors, amounts) flag entries that look wrong before the close, with approve and un-approve per item.
- **Hands-off data intake.** The accounting system already emailed daily GL exports. A watcher picks them up and loads them automatically, with an alert if an expected email doesn't arrive.
- **Month-end financial packets.** A per-location packet generated from the same data: income statement against budget, balance sheet, cash flow, AR and AP aging, and occupancy, as both a PDF and an Excel workbook.
- **Occupancy tracking.** A daily pull from the space-management platform for every location, backfilled for prior months and locked automatically at 8pm ET on the last day of each month so close numbers don't drift.

- All 5 locations roll up into one live dashboard, refreshed daily.
- Financial packets exist for every location and every month from January through August 2026, generated from the same data the dashboard shows.
